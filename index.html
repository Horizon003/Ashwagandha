Historical Background

Used in Ayurveda for over 3000 years. Mentioned in Charaka Samhita and Sushruta Samhita.

The name means “smell of horse”, symbolizing strength and vitality. Classified as a Rasayana (rejuvenative tonic).

Chemical Constituents

  • Withanolides (Withaferin A, Withanolide D) – main active compounds
  • Alkaloids (Somniferine, Anaferine)
  • Glycosides (Withanosides, Sitoindosides)
  • Flavonoids & sterols

Withanolides are responsible for most pharmacological activities.

CONCLUSION

Withania somnifera is an important medicinal plant of the Solanaceae family. Its rich withanolide content supports both traditional Ayurvedic uses and modern pharmacological applications, especially in stress management and immune support.
